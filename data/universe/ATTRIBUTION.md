# Universe snapshot attribution

This directory contains a frozen snapshot of the S&P 500 constituents derived from:
- Wikipedia: “List of S&P 500 companies” (retrieved: 2025-10-27)
- URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_S%26P_500_companies
- License: CC BY-SA 4.0

The CSV includes factual fields (`ticker`, `name`, `sector`, etc.) extracted by
`scripts/get_sp500_tickers.py`. No Wikipedia text is redistributed.
